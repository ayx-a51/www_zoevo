Goals

02Goals that fit

Aim for more. Cap what you want less of.

Every goal has a direction. Aim for at least 30 minutes of reading, or set a maximum you want to stay under, like two hours of screen time or a few cups of coffee.

  • Minimum (Aim) or Maximum (Cap). Landing exactly on a cap is still a success; one more is a breach. Cap marks turn amber, then orange, then red as you get close.
  • Stretch goals. Give a Minimum goal a second, higher target. Standout days show up in a richer green.
  • Planned days and reminders. Pick the weekdays that count. Unplanned days never count against you, and a reminder can nudge you at your planned time.
  • Daily and weekly. Weekly goals add up across the whole week, or count the days you completed one of your dailies.

Routines

04Routines

Routines that walk you through, step by step.

Line up a morning, a workout or a wind-down, and zoevo plays it back one step at a time with the clock running.

  1. warm-upTimed block, skipped
  2. runStopwatch, done
  3. push-upsTally, current step, 12 of 20
  4. cool downTimed block, up next
  • Four kinds of steps: timed blocks, tallies, focus sessions and stopwatches.
  • Progress counts toward your goals automatically. Every +1 and every finished focus session is logged the moment it happens.
  • Pause and continue later the same day, right where you left off.
  • Start blank or from a template: wake-up, commute, evening, bedtime and more in small, medium or large, plus a weekly review and a weekend reset.

Progress

05See your progress

Streaks, success rates and the whole story.

Every goal’s week at a glance, with streak flames that change color as they grow and a success rate that rewards bonus days.

  • Calendar history and charts for every habit, plus run history for routines: lifetime runs, average duration and when you usually run them.
  • Mark a day skipped or missed, and backfill what you forgot to log.
  • Unplanned wins count as a bonus, so a good week can top 100%.

Plan

06Plan your week

Shape next week before it starts.

Travel, a packed Tuesday, a long weekend: open Plan the week and choose which daily goals and routines count on which day.

  • A “plan now” button appears at the end of each week. Hold it to dismiss it for the week or turn it off.
  • Highlight special days, then plan or clear a whole day in one tap, or adjust single goals and routines.
  • Changes save instantly. Add an optional planning reminder and start your week on any day you like.
  • Away for a day? Unplan it rather than skip it. If you do the habit anyway, it still counts.

07Everyday details

The small things that make it stick.

A setup wizard that listens

Pick from twelve interests, say how much time you have on weekdays and weekends, and choose the parts of your day to anchor. zoevo suggests dailies, weeklies and routines you can edit before anything is created. Or start from examples, or from nothing.

Simple or Advanced

Advanced shows every mark, ring and number. Simple trims each tile to an icon and a name and lists only what’s still to do today, with the rest one tap away.

Home-screen widget

Link a widget to a tally to add one, to a stopwatch to start or stop it, or to a focus session to start one, in a single tap from your home screen. zoevo opens to confirm.

Sync, or stay a guest

zoevo works fully offline without an account. Sign in with Google to back up your data and pick up where you left off on another device.

10FAQ

Questions, answered.

Want the full tour? The manual walks through every screen, with how-tos and troubleshooting.

Read the manual
Is zoevo free?

Yes. zoevo is free to download and use, with every way to track, routines, the Timeline, the widget and sync. The free plan covers up to 5 daily goals, 3 weekly goals and 1 routine at a time.

zoevo+ is an optional subscription that raises those limits to 99, unlocks your complete history and every icon and color. You can try it free for 14 days.

Do I need an account? Does it work offline?

No account needed. As a guest, zoevo works fully offline and keeps your data on your phone. Sign in with Google to back up your data and sync it across devices; it keeps working offline when signed in, too.

Buying zoevo+, redeeming a promo code and restoring a purchase need a Google sign-in. The free trial doesn’t.

Does zoevo have ads or track me?

No. zoevo has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. As a guest, nothing you track is sent to us; if you sign in, your data syncs to your own account through Google Firebase. The privacy policy has the details.

Is zoevo available for iPhone?

No. zoevo is only available for Android, on Google Play.

Can I track habits I want to do less of?

Yes. Every goal has a direction. Minimum (Aim) is a target to reach, like at least 30 minutes of reading; reaching it exactly counts.

Maximum (Cap) is a limit to stay under, like at most two hours of screen time or two coffees. Landing exactly on the cap is still fine; one more is a breach, shows as missed right away and starts a daily’s streak over. Because you could still go over, a Cap day only turns green once the day is over.

Do reminders and timers work when the app is closed?

Yes. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, and running focus sessions and stopwatches stay in your notification shade with Pause and Continue. Allow notifications, and allow Alarms & reminders so a focus session ends right on time.

Goal reminders can arrive a few minutes late, because Android delivers them in batches to save battery. If they don’t arrive at all, exclude zoevo from battery restrictions.

Does the focus timer handle breaks?

Not automatically. A focus session is one block of a set length, 25 minutes unless you change it, and each finished session counts as one. Take your break, then start the next session.

What happens when my trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. If you have more goals or routines than the free plan allows, the newest ones beyond the limit are locked: still visible, but read-only. Archive or delete something to unlock the next one, or upgrade to unlock everything.

How do I cancel zoevo+?

Subscriptions run through Google Play, so you manage and cancel them in the Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

What about my data and privacy?

Our privacy policy explains what zoevo stores and why. In the app, the Data deletion section of the Account screen lets you clear your timeline, delete all your data or delete your account. You can also request deletion by email.
